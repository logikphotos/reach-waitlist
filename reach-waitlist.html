How it works

Getting started with REACH

We make the waitlist process as simple and transparent as possible for families.

1

Submit your intake form

Complete a brief form with your child's information and reason for seeking therapy. Takes about 5 minutes.

2

Receive your access code

You'll get a unique code by email or text. Use it anytime to check your position and estimated wait time.

3

We stay in touch

Every few weeks we'll check in to confirm you still need services. A simple reply keeps your spot.

4

We call when it's your turn

When a slot opens, our team will reach out directly to schedule your child's first appointment.

Ready to get started?

Adding your child to the waitlist takes less than 5 minutes. You'll receive a confirmation and access code right away.
